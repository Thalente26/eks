1

Grab Your Card & Register in 2 Steps

Pick up a free physical eKard at any partner shop or claim a digital card online. Link your number in two quick steps and your profile is live.

2

Shop Anywhere in the Network

Buy groceries at your spaza, refill water, get a haircut, or fix your car. Show your card number at the counter before you pay.

3

Earn Points Automatically

The cashier logs your purchase total and card number. Your points reflect straight onto your shopper profile-every R10 spent earns 1 point.

4

Redeem at Any Partner Store

Points aren't withdrawn as cash - they work as real store value across the network. Got 20 points? Walk into any partner shop, grab R20 worth of items, redeem your points, and walk out.

Simple Math: R10 = 1 Point (1 Pt = R1 Value)

Your points never get eaten by bank charges. Save them up across different shops and spend them whenever you need essentials.
